FAQ

Shipping from China to Ethiopia, answered

Sea freight (LCL or FCL) takes 38–44 days including transit through Djibouti and inland delivery. Rail + road is 18–24 days. Air freight is 4–6 days, and door-to-door express is 5–8 days. Your exact transit time is confirmed in your quote.

Sea freight LCL is almost always the cheapest — from $2.60/kg for consolidated cargo. If you're shipping more than roughly 12 CBM, a full 20ft container (from $3,200) becomes more economical. Rail is a strong middle option at $4.40/kg.

Yes. SilkBridge is a licensed, bonded customs broker in Djibouti and Ethiopia. We classify your goods, pre-calculate duties and taxes (payable in ETB), and clear your shipment at Modjo Dry Port or Bole International Airport.

We can't ship narcotics, weapons, counterfeit goods, or hazardous materials without proper certification. Electronics, machinery, textiles, construction materials, food and most commercial goods are fine — our team will advise on documentation per item type.

We accept USD, ETB and CNY via bank transfer. Standard terms are 30% deposit to book your space, with the balance due before departure. Customs duties and inland delivery fees can be settled in ETB after arrival.
