Our story

Two countries, one bridge — built on trust

Keraj Trading was founded on a simple observation: Ethiopian importers were being failed by forwarders who treated their cargo as a container number, not a business. We built the lane they deserved.

Our mission

To make importing from China to Ethiopia as reliable as ordering locally — one accountable team, transparent pricing, and every shipment visible from supplier door to final delivery.

Our vision

A connected Ethiopia where Chinese goods, machinery and technology move freely — with logistics as a growth engine for Ethiopian businesses, not a bottleneck.

Since 2014

Started with one container of textiles and a promise

In 2014, our founders — an Ethiopian freight veteran and a Chinese logistics operator — moved their first LCL shipment from Yiwu to Addis Ababa. It arrived damaged, three weeks late, with surprise fees.

They decided that would never happen to their clients. Today Keraj Trading runs weekly sailings, daily airlifts and a bonded warehouse network across three countries — but the promise hasn't changed: you always know where your cargo is and what it costs.

Milestones

A decade of building the China–Ethiopia lane

2014

The first shipment

Two founders, one LCL container from Yiwu, and a promise: transparent pricing and honest tracking, always.

2016

Weekly LCL consolidation

Fixed weekly sailings from Ningbo and Shenzhen to Djibouti. Our first 100 regular clients — mostly textile importers in Addis' Merkato.

2018

Customs brokerage license

Became a licensed, bonded broker in Ethiopia and Djibouti. Duties pre-calculated in every quote from then on.

2020

Air freight & express launch

Added daily air cargo and door-to-door express to meet demand for urgent parts and e-commerce parcels. Survived the pandemic together with our clients.

2022

Modjo bonded warehouse

Opened 8,000 m² of bonded storage at Modjo Dry Port, allowing clients to defer duties and improve cash flow.

2024

10 years · 15,000+ shipments

Crossed 15,000 shipments and opened the Addis Bole Lemi distribution centre. Named among Ethiopia's fastest-growing logistics firms.

2026

Full digital tracking

Every container and parcel now streams live GPS data with WhatsApp milestone alerts — the promise, automated.
