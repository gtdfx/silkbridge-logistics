Milestones

A decade of building the China–world bridge

2014

Founded in Guangzhou

Started as a sourcing agent helping importers find reliable Chinese suppliers — beginning with electronics and textiles.

2016

Factory audit program launched

Introduced on-site factory inspections and detailed audit reports — becoming one of the first sourcing agents to offer this service.

2018

Private label division opened

Expanded into OEM/ODM services — managing the full lifecycle from prototype to delivered branded products.

2020

Quality control center established

Built a dedicated QC team with AQL inspection standards — three-stage quality checks for every order.

2023

500+ verified suppliers

Reached 500 verified Chinese manufacturers across electronics, construction, machinery, medical and consumer goods.

2026

End-to-end sourcing + logistics

Now a full-service sourcing and import-export agent — from finding the supplier to delivering to your door, anywhere in the world.
