  • Transit

    38–44 days door-to-door, including Djibouti discharge, rail to Modjo and final trucking.

    Container booking, export docs, port handling, customs at both ends, delivery to your city.

03 · Rail + road

Intermodal via the Djibouti–Ethiopia railway

Sea freight to Djibouti, then the modern electric railway to Modjo Dry Port — the smart balance of cost and speed. Final-mile trucking connects Modjo to every Ethiopian city.

  • Transit

    18–24 days door-to-door — nearly half the time of pure sea routing.

  • Modjo Dry Port

    Dedicated bonded yard, customs on site, direct truck connections.

  • Weekly trains

    Fixed weekly block-train slots — predictable, trackable, on schedule.

We are a licensed, bonded customs broker in Djibouti and Ethiopia. We classify your goods, pre-calculate duties and taxes, and submit documentation before your cargo even arrives.

  • Pre-clearance

    Documents filed while cargo is in transit — goods typically clear within 24 hours of arrival.

  • Duty estimation

    HS classification and duty/tax estimates in your quote — payable in ETB before release.
