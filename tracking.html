What each status means

Milestones on the China → Ethiopia journey

Picked Up

Cargo collected from your Chinese supplier. Packing verified, export documents filed, container or air waybill issued. Your tracking number is live from this moment.

In Transit

Your cargo is moving — on the vessel, in the air or on the rail. GPS position and milestone updates appear here daily, with alerts on any schedule change.

Customs

Clearance at Djibouti Port, Modjo Dry Port or Bole Airport. Documents were pre-filed while in transit, so most shipments clear within 24 hours of arrival.

Delivered

Final-mile trucking complete. A proof of delivery (POD) is available on request, and our team follows up within 48 hours to confirm everything arrived in order.

Typical timelines

What to expect, per mode

Transit times are door-to-door from a Chinese supplier to an Ethiopian address. They include pickup, export, ocean/air/rail leg, customs and final delivery.

38–44 days door-to-door. Breakdown: 5–7 days China side (pickup + export), ~30 days ocean to Djibouti, 2–3 days rail to Modjo, 2–4 days customs + trucking to final city.

18–24 days. Ocean to Djibouti (~21 days) then priority rail slot to Modjo and trucking. Great balance of cost and speed for mid-size cargo.

Air freight: 4–6 days. Express: 5–8 days. Both include same-day pickup options in Guangzhou/Shenzhen, pre-cleared customs at Bole Airport and door delivery.
